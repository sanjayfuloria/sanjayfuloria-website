About

The work and the
why behind it

I have spent two decades studying how technology reshapes institutions — and how institutions shape technology adoption back. That tension is where I live. I joined IFHE Hyderabad to teach in the MBA programme, and over time took on responsibility for the Centre for Distance and Online Education, which oversees learning for thousands of students who cannot be in a classroom every day.

My research began with mobile banking adoption and has tracked the digital wave through blockchain, AI, and now agentic systems. I am interested less in the hype cycle and more in the institutional conditions that determine whether a technology creates genuine value or merely disruption. That scepticism — paired with genuine excitement about what is possible — shapes how I teach, write, and advise.

Outside the institution, I write for a broader audience on education policy, AI governance, and the future of management education in India. I believe academics have an obligation to participate in public discourse, not just produce papers for credentialed readers.
